# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want gatepack to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, including pull requests or issues made only to gain contribution counts.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately through GitHub's "Report content" feature or to the maintainers via a
private security advisory on this repository. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or pull requests, and may block contributors who
break this code.
